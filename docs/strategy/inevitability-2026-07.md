# The inevitability exercise — 2026-07-06

> Working backwards from a 2031 where Prism is a $100M ARR company. A narrative device, not
> a forecast: it exists to rank which of today's decisions matter. Several conclusions were
> promoted into [operating-principle.md](operating-principle.md) and [next-6-months.md](next-6-months.md).

## Why now (the five conditions, converged 2025–2027)

1. **AI became a budget line** boards ask about by name.
2. **Every vendor grades its own homework** — the conflict is structural and universally felt.
3. **CFO involvement arrived before measurement standards existed.** Cloud took ~8 years from first AWS bill to FinOps-as-discipline; AI compressed it to ~2. The referee seat is empty now.
4. **Work started generating its own audit trail** — agentic coding is the first knowledge work that logs itself. Measurement became *possible* at the moment justification became *mandatory*. (The underrated one.)
5. **Multi-vendor became permanent procurement strategy** — vendor-native dashboards can never be the system of record.

The window = the gap between condition 3 appearing and an incumbent noticing. That's also the clock.

## The ten answers (condensed)

1. **Market truth people underestimated:** AI spend never became *trusted* spend — the line item stays contested every cycle because vendors keep churning and keep self-grading. The referee demand is structural, not transitional.
2. **Assumption the founders got right early:** trust, not insight, is the product. Honest nulls and confidence gates looked like pedantry; they were the only properties letting a number survive into a board deck. And determinism-with-LLM-narrate-only was architecture before the industry's fabricated-metrics scandals made it a requirement.
3. **What customers realized two years later:** they weren't buying measurement — they were buying the provenance record of how software gets built. **ROI was the reason to install; provenance was the reason they could never uninstall.**
4. **PRD decisions that became foundations:** the link ladder with method+confidence per edge (became the graph's edge schema — the drill-down became the API); evidence tiers everywhere; the first-party `pr-link` event (instrument-the-agent pattern); the adoption loop (became the verified-delta product); isolated lanes (graph as shared substrate, products as views).
5. **Roadmap parts that proved unnecessary:** composite indexes and bands; the harness *score* (the annotations mattered); the wizard; the manager index; packs-as-product; most of the coaching rule engine. **Pattern: everything that computed a judgment aged badly; everything that recorded evidence became foundation.**
6. **Feature that accidentally became the platform:** the link ledger → the provenance API queried by CI, security, compliance, insurers.
7. **Irreplicable data asset:** the intervention-outcome corpus — can't be backfilled, scraped, or bought; by 2029 it powered recommendations with cited base rates.
8. **Non-obvious network effect:** the **referee effect** — vendors emit provenance voluntarily because un-instrumented work renders as the "unattributed" gray slice in their customer's chart, and "Prism-verified" became the trust currency of AI tooling deals.
9. **Competitor mistake that let Prism win:** every platform chose self-interest over neutrality (GitHub Copilot-biased, vendors self-graded, Datadog shipped always-a-number attribution and got publicly burned). Their inability to say "insufficient data" was a business-model constraint, not a feature gap.
10. **The roadmap rewritten by someone who knows the ending** → adopted as [next-6-months.md](next-6-months.md): install the ledger early (tenure is the moat's clock), one wedge artifact, lossy multi-tool sooner than comfortable, close one loop fast, cut everything that computes judgments.

## Standing insight

**"The graph only becomes a moat if it's installed early"** — the product/moat/distribution/
timing chain in one sentence. Every month without design partners is a month the moat's
accumulation rate is zero.
