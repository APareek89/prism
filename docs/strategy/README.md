## Standing meta-rules (the process lessons, learned the hard way)

1. **No new strategy abstractions until the first verified delta exists.** The philosophical questions are answered; reopening them is procrastination with good production values.
2. **Every new artifact carries a kill criterion** — a document that can't fail is decoration.
3. **The correctness trap is our house failure mode:** this team (founder + Claude) naturally optimizes for internally-perfect systems before customer contact. When in doubt, choose the option that touches a customer sooner.
4. **Sequencing tiebreaker:** when two options are close, pick the one with the shorter verification window — it builds the corpus faster.
5. The 12-month roadmap and its PRDs remain the **build library** — pulled from as experiments justify, never executed on autopilot.
